ScamGuard Privacy Policy

ScamGuard is a personal tool used only by the members of one family on their own computer.

  • It requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly) solely to check the family's own messages for scam indicators.
  • Message data is processed and stored only on the family's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

ScamGuard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
